Exposure

Exposure

The process of shining UV light through a film positive onto an emulsion-coated screen to permanently burn a stencil into the mesh.

Flood Stroke

Flood Stroke

A gentle pull of the squeegee while the screen is lifted off the substrate, coating the mesh with ink to prevent water-based inks from drying out and clogging open areas.

Halftone

Halftone

A visual technique that breaks solid tones or gradients into small, varied-size dots, creating the illusion of continuous tone when printed with a single ink color.

Mesh Count

Mesh Count

The number of threads crossing per square inch of screen fabric. Lower counts (e.g., 110) let thicker ink through for fabric, while higher counts (e.g., 230) hold fine details for paper.

Squeegee

Squeegee

A flexible rubber or polyurethane blade set in a handle, used to force ink through the open mesh of a screen onto a substrate.

Washout

Washout

Rinsing a newly exposed screen with water to wash away unexposed, soft emulsion, revealing the open mesh stencil beneath.
